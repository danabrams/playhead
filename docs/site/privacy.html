Privacy Policy

Playhead. Last updated 2 September 2026.

Playhead analyses podcasts on your iPhone. Episode audio and the transcripts derived from it never leave your device, and there is no Playhead server for them to go to.

What never leaves your device

  • Episode audio. Downloads are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Transcripts. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device. No audio is sent to a transcription service.
  • What Playhead decides about an episode — where the ad breaks are, what it skipped, what it showed you a card for.
  • Your listening. Playback positions, what you played and when, and the corrections you make are stored on your device only.

Playhead has no user accounts and operates no servers. We do not collect, receive, or store your listening history, and we have no way to look at it.

What does leave your device

Three things, each with a different destination. None is Playhead's server, because there is not one.

Crash and hang reports go through Apple's own system, subject to whether you have opted in to sharing them with developers in iOS Settings. Playhead contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software of any kind.

Diagnostic reports

If something goes wrong you can send us a diagnostic report from Settings. It is attached to an email you compose, so you can open it and read it before sending. Nothing is transmitted unless you send that email.

A report describes how the app's own machinery behaved. It contains no audio, no transcripts, no episode titles and no free text you or a podcast wrote. Where an episode has to be referred to at all, it is identified by an irreversible hash rather than by name, so one report can be read consistently without naming what you listened to.

This is enforced in the code rather than promised in this document. The report is built by a component that admits only a fixed vocabulary of values, and an automated test populates every free-text-capable field with a marker and fails the build if any marker appears anywhere in the encoded report.

Usage statistics

Playhead keeps a small number of counters on your device — how many ad cards were shown, how many you confirmed, how often you reached for the skip button. They are not uploaded. No mechanism to send them is switched on in the app you can install, and they exist so the app can be improved from reports people choose to send, not collected in the background.

If that ever changes it will be described here first, and it will not apply retroactively to counters recorded under this policy.

Children

Playhead is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing about any user, this is a statement about intent rather than a data-handling practice.

Your choices

  • Turn off iCloud sync in Playhead's Settings to stop your subscription list syncing to your iCloud account.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device, including downloads, transcripts and analysis.
  • Delete your iCloud data for Playhead through iOS Settings under your Apple Account, which removes the synced subscription list.
  • Send nothing. Diagnostic reports are entirely optional and the app works identically without them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and describe the change in the app's release notes. The version in effect is the one published here.
