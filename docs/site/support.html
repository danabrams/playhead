Common questions

Why did nothing get skipped?
Playhead only skips a break when it is certain, and certainty comes from comparing two copies of the same episode. Some podcast networks serve every listener an identical file, and on those shows there is nothing to compare, so Playhead falls back to showing you a card rather than guessing. A wrong skip takes away the thing you came for, and a card does not.
Why is there a card instead of a skip?
Because Playhead was not sure. A host talking warmly about a sponsor halfway into a story is genuinely ambiguous, and the card lets you decide in one tap. What you choose is remembered for that show.
An episode is still preparing. How long does it take?
Preparation runs in the background, usually while your phone is charging. An episode downloaded overnight is normally ready by morning. Keeping the app open and on power is the fastest way to finish one immediately.
It skipped part of the show. What do I do?
Tap to jump back, and mark it as not an ad. That correction outranks everything Playhead decided on its own and is remembered for that show. Please also send a diagnostic report — a wrong skip is the failure we most want to see.
Which iPhones does it work on?
An iPhone with Apple Intelligence, running a current version of iOS. The speech recognition and analysis run on the device, which is what that hardware is for. English-language shows only, for now.
Can I use my existing subscriptions?
Yes. Search for a show or add it by RSS address. Your subscription list syncs through your own iCloud account, so it follows you between devices; you can turn that off in Settings.
Does it upload my audio or listening history?
No. Episode audio and the transcripts made from it never leave your iPhone, there are no Playhead accounts and no Playhead servers, and there is no third-party tracking or analytics in the app. The privacy policy sets out the three things that do leave the device and where each one goes.
How do I delete everything?
Deleting the app removes every download, transcript and piece of analysis from the device. Your synced subscription list can be removed through iOS Settings, under your Apple Account.

Reporting a problem well

The most useful report is: the show and roughly where in the episode, what you expected, what happened, and a diagnostic report from Settings. If the problem is a wrong skip, say whether you tapped to jump back — that tells us whether the correction path worked.
